# Applied Industrial Technologies (applied-industrial-technologies)

Applied Industrial Technologies is a leading industrial distributor of bearings, power transmission products, fluid power components, industrial rubber products, linear motion components, tools, and related industrial supplies. They serve maintenance, repair, and operations (MRO) customers across manufacturing, food processing, transportation, and other industries.
